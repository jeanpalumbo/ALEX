# Kit de arranque — cuenta de X en inglés (Plan B)

> Todo en inglés, público de EE. UU. Piero publica a mano; nada se automatiza.
> Revisar y retocar cada post antes de publicar (los pagos de X excluyen contenido automatizado).

## 1. Pasos para crear la cuenta (Piero, ~15 min)

1. Crear un correo nuevo solo para la cuenta (p. ej. un Gmail nuevo). No usar el personal.
2. Registrarse en x.com **desde la web**, idioma de la cuenta: English. País: el real.
3. Elegir nombre y @usuario de la lista de abajo (comprobar cuál está libre).
4. Foto de perfil y cabecera: sencillas (pedirlas a ChatGPT con los prompts de abajo).
5. Pegar la bio, publicar el post de presentación y **fijarlo**.
6. Seguir a 30-50 cuentas grandes de los 6 nichos (sirve para que X entienda de qué va la cuenta).
7. Premium: contratarlo **desde la web**, solo cuando se active Grok Bot / Trend Scout.

## 3. Bio (máx. 160 caracteres)

> One rabbit hole a day: fun facts, short stories and the tech shaping what's next. Written by a human, with a little help from AI.

(Decir que hay ayuda de IA es honesto y evita problemas si alguien pregunta.)

## 4. Post de presentación (fijar)

> • a fun fact you'll want to tell someone

> • something new in tech & AI, explained simply
>

## 5. Diez posts de ejemplo (para ver el tono; revisa y retoca antes de publicar)

**Fun facts** (hechos comprobados; si algún dato te suena raro, quítalo):

1. > Octopuses have three hearts.
   > Two pump blood to the gills. The third pumps it to the rest of the body — and it stops beating while they swim.
   > Which might be why they'd rather crawl.

2. > Botanically speaking, a banana is a berry.
   > A strawberry is not.
   > Nobody asked botany for its opinion, but here we are.

3. > The Eiffel Tower gets taller in summer.
   > Heat makes the iron expand, so it can grow by around 15 cm (6 inches) on a hot day.
   > Even buildings relax a little in summer.

**Story** (ficción, siempre marcada):

4. > Story 🧵 (fiction)
   > My grandfather kept one locked drawer his whole life.
   > When he died, we finally opened it.
   > Inside: 52 letters, one for every year of his marriage, each addressed to my grandmother.
   > None were ever sent.
   > The last one said: "I never needed to. You read me anyway."

**Tech / AI** (opinión y consejos, sin cifras inventadas):

5. > The most useful AI prompt I know isn't "write this for me."
   > It's: "Here's my plan. Argue against it as hard as you can."
   > You'll find the holes before anyone else does.

6. > AI won't replace your job.
   > But "I'll do it tomorrow" is about to lose its best excuse.
   > Tasks that took an afternoon now take 10 minutes. The hard part is deciding what's worth doing.

**Sports** (pregunta para conversación, sin datos de actualidad):

7. > Settle this once and for all:
   > Is it harder to hit a 95 mph fastball or return a 130 mph serve?
   > Wrong answers only… then right ones.

**True crime / misterios** (historia documentada, contada con prudencia):

8. > In December 1872, a ship called the Mary Celeste was found drifting in the Atlantic.
   > Sails partly set. Cargo almost untouched. Plenty of food and water.
   > Not a single person aboard.
   > More than 150 years later, nobody knows for sure what happened. 🧵

**Build in public** (nicho nuevo, inspirado en el formato de "Robbie" pero con datos reales tuyos):

9. > Day 1 of trying to build small online income streams with AI — in public, with real numbers.
   > First project: a large-print word search book for seniors. AI helped write it; I review everything.
   > Cost so far: $0. Sales so far: 0.
   > I'll post every win and every flop.

10. > Things AI did well this week: drafted 100 puzzle themes, built the PDF, checked every answer.
    > Things it couldn't do: open my bank account, decide the price, or tell me if my grandma would actually like it.
    > Still a human's job.

## 6. Trend Scout (pegar en Grok Bot cuando esté contratado)

**Nombre:** Trend Scout

```
You are Trend Scout. Your only job is to find what is taking off on X right now in these niches: short stories, fun facts, tech & AI, sports, true crime & mysteries, build in public (AI side hustles), in English (US audience).

Every day at 08:00 Europe/Madrid:
1. Search X for posts from the last 24h in the niches above with unusually high engagement relative to the author's normal posts.
2. Pick the 15 best. For each one, append a row to the Google Sheet "Trend Scout", tab "trends", with columns:
   date | niche | topic | format (thread/single/image/video/poll) | hook_pattern (describe the hook style, do NOT copy text) | why_it_works | post_url | views | likes | reposts | lang
3. Never copy the full text of any post into the sheet. Describe the pattern instead.
4. Do not post, like, follow, reply or DM on X. Read only.
5. If X or the Sheet is unavailable, write one row with niche="ERROR" and the reason, then stop.
```

**Google Sheet:** crear una hoja llamada **Trend Scout** con dos pestañas:
- `trends` → importar `trends_sheet_template.csv` (solo trae las cabeceras).
- `drafts` → importar `drafts_sheet_template.csv` (aquí escribirá el sistema los borradores).

## 8. Ritmo de las 2 primeras semanas

- 3-5 posts al día, repartidos entre los 6 nichos, a mano.
- Mejores franjas para EE. UU.: 8-10 h, 12-13 h y 19-21 h hora del este (14-16 h, 18-19 h y 1-3 h en España). Programarlos desde la web de X.
- Responder a comentarios con calma; no pedir likes ni reposts (X lo penaliza).
- Final de la semana 2: mirar impresiones por post de cada nicho y quedarnos con 1-2.
